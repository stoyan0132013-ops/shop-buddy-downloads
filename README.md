# SHOP BUDDY

**Your shopping companion for receipts, expenses, and shopping
information.**

[**Download SHOP BUDDY for Android ---
v1.0.0**](https://github.com/stoyan0132013-ops/shop-buddy-downloads/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

SHOP BUDDY helps you keep your shopping information organized on your
Android device.

## Features

-   **Receipt organization** --- keep track of shopping receipts.
-   **Expense tracking** --- review shopping expenses.
-   **Receipt scanning** --- use the app's text-recognition workflow
    where supported.
-   **Favorites** --- mark items or information you want to find again.
-   **Cloud sync** --- synchronize supported app data when you sign in
    and manually run sync.
-   **Local access** --- locally saved information can remain available
    on the device.

Feature availability can depend on app settings, permissions,
connectivity, and the data you have added.

## Data and privacy

SHOP BUDDY can store information locally and supports Firebase-backed
sign-in and synchronization. Data sent to cloud services depends on the
features you use and the app's current implementation. Receipt photos
are not necessarily uploaded just because receipt data syncs.

Do not add sensitive information you do not need. Keep your account
credentials private. A full privacy policy describing data collection,
processing, retention, and contact details should be published
separately before broad promotion.
