2. Information the app may handle
Depending on which features you use, SHOP BUDDY may process:
Account information: information used by Firebase Authentication
to create and manage your sign-in account, such as your email
address and authentication identifiers.
Shopping and receipt information: receipt details, expense
amounts, dates, notes, and related information you choose to enter
or scan.
Favorites and app records: items or records you mark or create
in the app.
Receipt images: images selected or captured for receipt
workflows. Images may be stored locally on your device. Do not
assume an image is uploaded to the cloud just because related
receipt details synchronize.
Technical and diagnostic information: Firebase or Android
components may process technical information required to provide,
protect, and troubleshoot their services. The exact information
depends on the SDKs and configuration in the released version.
Do not enter payment-card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive
information into receipt notes unless the app specifically supports and
protects that information.
3. Where information is stored
SHOP BUDDY stores some app information locally on your device. If you
sign in and use cloud synchronization, supported records may be sent to
Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud
Firestore, according to the app's implementation and the Firebase
security rules.
Local receipt images and cloud-synchronized receipt records may be
stored separately. Keep your own backup of important information.
Uninstalling the app, clearing app storage, changing devices, or losing
account access can affect locally stored information.
Developer verification required: confirm the exact fields
synchronized by the released version and the Firebase products actually
enabled. Update this section if the implementation differs.
4. Why information is used
Information is used to provide the app's features, including sign-in,
receipt and shopping-record management, favorites, optional
synchronization, and troubleshooting or protecting the service.
We do not claim that information is used for advertising or sold to
advertisers. Any future advertising, analytics, or other third-party
processing must be reflected in this policy before it is introduced.
